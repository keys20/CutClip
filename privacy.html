Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

CutClip is a Windows desktop application for creating and preparing short videos. This policy describes the intended data handling for the locally installed application. The operator must confirm these practices before publishing this policy.

Information processed

CutClip may process video URLs, downloaded video files, audio transcriptions, clip metadata, scheduled publishing entries, upload history, and account identifiers and OAuth tokens when you connect a supported platform.

Purpose and storage

Data is processed to create clips, generate descriptions and hashtags, manage a local publishing queue, authenticate authorized accounts, and initiate uploads you request. Application data and credentials are stored on your device, subject to your device security and the app's implementation.

Sharing with third parties

When you explicitly initiate an upload, the selected video and related data are transmitted to the authorized platform, such as TikTok. The platform processes the information under its own policies. Video sources and AI model services may have separate data practices. Do not claim fully offline processing unless verified in the installed version.

Retention and deletion

Locally stored clips and records remain until deleted by the user or the application under its configured retention behavior. To request deletion of connected-account information, disconnect the account and contact the operator for instructions. Token revocation and local deletion must be verified in the application.

Security

Protect your Windows account and application data folder. Do not share OAuth tokens or credentials. No security method can guarantee absolute protection.

Your choices

You may choose whether to connect a platform and whether to submit a video. You may revoke platform authorization using the platform's account settings.
