Windows desktop application

Make short clips and prepare them for sharing.

CutClip is a local video clipping application that uses FFmpeg, audio transcription and local AI to help users prepare short-form videos and metadata.

With authorized integrations, users can connect their social accounts and explicitly submit selected clips to supported platforms. Platform features depend on permissions and approvals.

How it works

  1. Provide a video source you have the right to use.
  2. Generate and review local clips and suggested metadata.
  3. Select a supported platform and authorize access.
  4. Review and initiate an upload; complete publishing on the platform when required.
